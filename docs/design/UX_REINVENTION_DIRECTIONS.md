Mode: Operate. A Telegram user needs to compare a question, available opposing
offers and an intentional transaction. Funded P2P orders determine execution;
the platform does not supply odds. Fixtures are labelled synthetic and use no API.

Baseline-driven improvements to validate: question/search outrank aggregate totals;
missing offers have words; long economics follows amount entry; explicit review

After independent A/B review and scoped correction verdicts, Pocket is selected
for the experimental implementation: its conditions-first path, contextual trade
panel and reachable navigation best support the beginner phone use case. Folio
and Exchange remain full interactive alternatives. This is not owner approval for
production. Real API integration is the next stage, not a prototype claim.
